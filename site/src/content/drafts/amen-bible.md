## Overview

Amen.bible is a multi-language Bible app. I worked on it with a small team and owned the user interface, from design to implementation.

## My role

- Designed the interface and the reading experience
- Built the UI in Flutter, covering web and mobile from one codebase
- Made the layout hold up across more than 23 languages, including long strings and different scripts

## What it taught me

Building for many languages from the start forces cleaner components and fewer hard-coded assumptions.
