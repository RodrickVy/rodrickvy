## Overview

A contract project for a lifestyle-medicine intervention and education program in South Africa. They needed a site for a course event, and I built it from design to launch.

## What I built

- Designed and prototyped the site in Adobe XD
- Built it with **Angular** and **TypeScript**
- Added **Firebase** authentication, storage, and database for user accounts
- Built a small CMS so the team could manage course content without me

## What it taught me

Handling real user accounts made me think carefully about authentication and access rules, which is part of what led me toward security.
